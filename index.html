Common questions

Can't find your answer? Get in touch and we'll help.

Is $1,880 worth it?

Here's what the $1,880 covers: 40 hours of instructor-led coaching in a cohort of three, individual feedback on your work, practice files, objective checklists, timed mock exams, your MOS exam voucher with 1 extra retake, and a free class retake if you don't pass. That works out to $47 per coaching hour, and you finish with a credential you can show on your CV and LinkedIn.

Do I need prior Excel experience?

The Associate programme assumes basic familiarity with Excel. The Expert programme is for those who already hold the Associate credential or have equivalent proficiency.

What happens if I don't pass?

First attempt: you are welcome to enrol in our class one more time at no additional cost, and your exam voucher also covers 1 extra exam retake.

Second attempt: this is our Pass-or-Get-50%-Back guarantee. If you complete the full programme and still don't pass after your second attempt, you may be eligible to receive 50% of your programme fee back. If you put in the work and make full use of the coaching, practice and support provided, we're willing to put 50% of your programme fee on the line with you.

How long does the programme take?

Both programmes require approximately 40 hours, typically completed over five days.
